# 12. LAB — Configure QoS

# Lab Goal

Classify, mark, queue, and rate-manage traffic according to policy.

# Tasks

1. Define voice and data classes.
2. Match traffic using supported header/application criteria.
3. Apply CoS or DSCP markings at the trust boundary.
4. Assign priority and weighted queues.
5. Apply a policing or shaping target.
6. Generate congestion and observe queues, drops, and delay.
7. Verify voice priority without starving data.

# Success Criteria

- [ ] Classification and marking match policy.
- [ ] Rate behavior matches policing or shaping intent.
- [ ] Lower queues retain usable service.
