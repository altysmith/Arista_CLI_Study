# 8. Introduction to QoS

# Learn

Congestion occurs when available bandwidth cannot transmit all queued packets. Best-effort forwarding may drop any traffic. QoS does not add bandwidth; it prioritizes and manages how bandwidth is used.

Key characteristics are bandwidth, one-way delay, round-trip time, jitter, and packet loss. The notes use VoIP targets of no more than 150 ms and less than 1% packet loss. TCP can retransmit; UDP itself does not.
