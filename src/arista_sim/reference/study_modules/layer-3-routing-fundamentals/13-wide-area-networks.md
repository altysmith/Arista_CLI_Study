# 13. Wide Area Networks

# Learn

A WAN connects separate LANs through service-provider infrastructure and routers. Routers sit at both sides of the WAN and forward between campus, branch, and data-center networks.

WAN-facing and LAN-facing interfaces may use different media and technologies. Multiple WAN paths allow routing protocols to select and fail over between routes.
