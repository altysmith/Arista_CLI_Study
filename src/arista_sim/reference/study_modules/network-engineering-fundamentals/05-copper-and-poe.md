# 5. Copper and PoE

**Official unit:** Network Engineering Fundamentals → Physical Layer

# Learn

Copper Ethernet sends electrical signals and has a 100 m maximum length in the notes. UTP twists eight wires to minimize EMI but has no shielding; STP adds EMI protection at higher cost. At lower speeds, two wires carry TX and two RX; higher speeds use all eight.

The T568B notes associate orange with TX and green with RX. Crossover cables connect devices directly. With four wires carrying TX/RX, the brown and blue wires provide PoE.

# Flashcards

**Copper maximum length?** 100 m.  
**Why twist pairs?** Minimize EMI.  
**UTP versus STP?** STP adds shielding and cost.
