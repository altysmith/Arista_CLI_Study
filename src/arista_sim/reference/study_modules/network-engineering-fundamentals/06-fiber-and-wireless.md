# 6. Fiber & Wireless

**Official unit:** Network Engineering Fundamentals → Physical Layer

# Learn

Fiber carries light and avoids EMI. Its buffer protects, cladding keeps light inside, and core carries it. SMF is yellow, has a small glass core, longer distance, and higher bandwidth. MMF is orange/blue, has a larger glass/plastic core, shorter distance, lower bandwidth, and lower cost.

Duplex fiber uses one TX and one RX strand. Parallel connections bundle fibers. SFPs insert into switches; DACs join compatible same-rack devices with attached SFP ends.

The notes associate 2.4 GHz with 802.11b/g/n, channels 1/6/11, higher power and lower speed; 5 GHz with 802.11a/n/ac, lower power, higher speed, and 25 non-overlapping channels.

# Flashcards

**SMF versus MMF?** SMF: longer/higher; MMF: shorter/lower and cheaper.  
**What is a DAC?** A pre-made same-rack cable with SFP ends.
