# 4. Introduction to VLANs

# Learn

VLANs replace purely physical separation with logical segmentation. Benefits include smaller broadcast scope, organization by purpose, isolation, easier changes, and efficient switch use.

```text
1 VLAN = 1 broadcast domain = 1 subnet
```

VLANs can extend across the Layer 2 fabric. VLAN 1 is the default, and switch interfaces are assigned to the desired numbered VLAN.

# Flashcards

**One VLAN equals?** One broadcast domain and one subnet.  
**Default VLAN?** VLAN 1.
